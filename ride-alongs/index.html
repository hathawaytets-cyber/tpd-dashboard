Civilian Observer Program · Florida State Roleplay

Civilian ride-alongs

Apply to accompany an available Tampa Police Department roleplay officer on a patrol. Applications are submitted and reviewed in the department Discord ticket system, not on this website.

Open application document Back to homepage

How to apply

  1. 01

    Make your own copy

    Open the application reference in Google Docs. Choose File → Make a copy, then complete your personal copy using roleplay information only.

  2. 02

    Open a civilian ride-along ticket

    In the TPD roleplay Discord server, open the designated civilian ride-along ticket and provide a link to your completed Google Doc copy. Set its sharing access so the ticket reviewers can read it.

  3. 03

    Wait for the review

    The application is reviewed in that Discord ticket. An available officer must be arranged, and their Sergeant or higher must approve the ride-along before it begins. Do not start without that approval.

Ticket review and approval

Open the designated civilian ride-along ticket inside the TPD roleplay Discord, share your completed copy with the reviewing staff, and follow all instructions in that ticket. The ticket is where the decision is communicated. The assigned officer’s Sergeant or higher must approve before participation; the Chief or designee may deny or revoke permission under Personnel Handbook §12.1.

Do not treat document completion, a ticket submission, or a website link as approval.

This is a fictional Roblox roleplay program, not the real Tampa Police Department’s Citizen Observer Program. The linked source is an adapted reference draft containing real-world form fields and legal wording. Never provide actual identity documents, birth dates, addresses, phone numbers, or emergency-contact information in roleplay applications or tickets. Use only roleplay-appropriate information and follow the roleplay department’s staff guidance.
