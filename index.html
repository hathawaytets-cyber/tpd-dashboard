Community · Civilian Ride-Alongs

Experience a patrol shift

In Florida State Roleplay, civilians can request to observe a TPD patrol. Complete a copy of the application, open a Discord ticket, and await a supervisor’s decision.

Open application Full instructions
  1. 1

    Make your own copy

    Open the application reference in Google Docs. Choose File → Make a copy, then complete your personal copy using roleplay information only.

  2. 2

    Open a civilian ride-along ticket

    In the TPD roleplay Discord server, open the designated civilian ride-along ticket and provide a link to your completed Google Doc copy. Set its sharing access so the ticket reviewers can read it.

  3. 3

    Wait for the review

    The application is reviewed in that Discord ticket. An available officer must be arranged, and their Sergeant or higher must approve the ride-along before it begins. Do not start without that approval.

Roleplay only. Never provide real personal identifiers or contact details in a Discord ticket. This adapted document is a reference draft, not an official City of Tampa application or legal waiver. Our core values

Personnel Handbook §2

  • 01

    Integrity

    Act honestly, ethically, and transparently in all duties.

  • 02

    Accountability

    Accept responsibility for actions and decisions, including mistakes.

  • 03

    Service

    Prioritize the safety and well-being of the community above all.

  • 04

    Professionalism

    Maintain appropriate conduct, appearance, and communication at all times.

  • 05

    Respect

    Treat all individuals with dignity, fairness, and impartiality.

Recruitment

From application to your first solo shift

Every officer follows the pathway in the Personnel Handbook (§3.1). New officers join as Probationary Patrol Officers and train with a Police Training Officer selected by the Office of the Chiefs, who then approve them for independent patrol.

Start your application
  1. 01

    Apply

    Complete the recruitment application. Successful applicants are appointed Probationary Patrol Officers.

  2. 02

    TPD onboarding

    Department structure and chain of command.

  3. 03

    Policy and procedures review

    Personnel Handbook and Use of Force Policy.

  4. 04

    AnchorCAD training

    Filing arrests, citations and incidents (HB §10).

  5. 05

    Radio communication training

    Call signs and radio procedure (HB §9.1).

  6. 06

    Patrol procedures training

    Patrol operations, pursuits and jurisdiction (HB §7).

  7. 07

    PTO Mentorship Program

    Training with a Police Training Officer selected and approved by the Office of the Chiefs.

  8. 08

    Independent patrol approval

    The Office of the Chiefs approves the officer for independent patrol and probation ends.
