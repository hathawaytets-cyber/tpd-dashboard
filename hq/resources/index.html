Weekly activity

HB §3.2

Three (3) hours of documented patrol activity per week, for all TPD personnel regardless of division or assignment. Divisions may not set their own quotas.

Exceptions

  • Approved Leave of Absence (LOA)
  • Department assignments
  • Training requirements
  • Personal circumstances approved by command staff
  • Other circumstances approved through the chain of command

Officers completing PTO are exempt until approved for independent patrol.
